Every task is timed against a target derived from the real budget: roughly seven minutes each, including reading the
prompt, switching context, and verifying. Start the clock before you read the scenario — that is the exam.

<p class="small muted">These are original scenarios written from the published curriculum weights and community
retrospectives. They are not exam questions and are not modelled on any specific one.</p>
